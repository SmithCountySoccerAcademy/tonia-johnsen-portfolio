About

Tonia Johnsen is an independent painter working in abstract and expressive forms. Each piece is one of a kind—built through layers of color, gesture, and quiet revision until the surface finds its balance.

Her work is intended for living spaces: calm enough to live with, strong enough to hold a wall. Available originals are ready for their next home. Commissions are welcome when a space calls for something made for it.
