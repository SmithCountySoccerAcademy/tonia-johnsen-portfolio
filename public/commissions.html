Commissions

Looking for a piece sized or colored for a specific room? Commissions begin with a short conversation about scale, palette, and mood. Each custom work is painted to order and delivered when complete.

Pricing depends on size and complexity. A deposit holds your place; the balance is due when the painting is complete.
